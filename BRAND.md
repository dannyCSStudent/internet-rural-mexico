# Internet Rural México — Brand Guide

## Locked identity

**Name:** Internet Rural México

**Tagline:** Internet para donde las opciones tradicionales no llegan.

**Supporting message:** Entiende tus opciones antes de gastar en Internet.

## Positioning

An independent rural-connectivity information and comparison resource. Starlink is the initial monetization channel through eligible referrals, but the brand is not a Starlink property and should remain useful if other technologies become better options.

## Audience

Rural property owners/users in Mexico, beginning in Tiquicheo/Michoacán: homes, casas de campo, fincas, ranchos, rural businesses, remote workers, camera users, families, students, and people with weak or nonexistent cellular coverage.

## Visual system

- Deep Green: `#24523A`
- Sky Blue: `#2F80C0`
- Warm Sand: `#E7D6B5`
- Charcoal: `#1F2933`
- Off White: `#F7F5EF`
- Typography: Inter or Geist Sans

Visual direction: trustworthy, rural, modern, simple, mobile-first. Avoid Starlink/SpaceX branding or imagery that could imply official affiliation.

## Voice

Knowledgeable local helping a neighbor. Explain costs, tradeoffs, limitations, and alternatives without hype.

## Required disclosure

> Este sitio es independiente de Starlink. Algunos enlaces son enlaces de referencia. Si realizas una compra elegible mediante uno de ellos, puedo recibir una recompensa de Starlink.

**H1:** Internet para donde las opciones tradicionales no llegan.
