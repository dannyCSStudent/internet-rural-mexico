# Internet Rural México — Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Referral setup

Complete: Starlink referral activated, cash reward method selected, Stripe onboarding/banking setup completed.

## Phase 1 — Business, audience, brand, research

Complete: rural Mexico focus, Tiquicheo/Michoacán research, customer segmentation, brand, MVP, SEO strategy.

## Phase 2 — Technical foundation

**Current phase.** Use `dannyCSStudent/business-app-starter-template` as the starting Turborepo. Convert its CRM-oriented active web experience into Internet Rural México without rebuilding the architecture from scratch.

## Phase 3 — Website MVP

Build the nine locked MVP routes, mobile-first design, SEO metadata, sitemap, robots, OG basics, structured data where useful, and referral disclosure.

## Phase 4 — Qualification tool

Build “¿Starlink es para mí?” as simple logic first; avoid a database unless requirements prove one is needed.

## Phase 5 — Content engine

Publish useful rural-connectivity content: rural Internet, ranchos/fincas, Starlink Mexico, total cost, equipment, rain, electricity, sky view, cameras, remote work, video calls, gaming, alternatives, and who should not buy Starlink.

## Phase 6 — Organic distribution

Prioritize Google, YouTube, Facebook groups/reels, then TikTok/Instagram. No spam or fake engagement.

## Phase 7 — SEO expansion

Start with Tiquicheo/Michoacán and expand only when genuine demand/evidence supports it. Do not create thin city pages.

## Phase 8 — Analytics

Measure landing pages, sources, qualification starts/completions, and referral CTA clicks. Add infrastructure only when useful.

## Phase 9 — Automation

Only after the funnel works. Do not build automation for its own sake.
